# 0100 — Sky view

## Purpose

The live sky: the dish's own obstruction survey drawn as a three-dimensional
dome, with the terminal standing at its centre. This ports the dome half of
`SatelliteView` from the Electron renderer to a native `gtk::GLArea` backed by
`glow`, and keeps every piece of geometry that can be tested free of GTK.

The survey is built from the status and obstruction replies the recorder now
polls (spec 0030). The view adds no poll of its own, and it never calls the
router.

## Requirements

- **SPEC-SKY-001** — A survey is built from the live obstruction map and status:
  a cell is unmapped, clear, partial, or obstructed using the same thresholds as
  the TypeScript app, and the boresight azimuth/elevation come from status.
- **SPEC-SKY-002** — The obstruction grid is oriented as the firmware reports:
  `FRAME_EARTH` is north-up, `FRAME_UT` is rotated by the boresight azimuth, and
  a missing frame assumes `FRAME_UT` for Mini kits and `FRAME_EARTH` otherwise.
- **SPEC-SKY-003** — The dome is a hemisphere of one point per in-radius grid
  cell. When unmapped trimming is on, only never-observed cells beyond the
  observed envelope are dropped; no reading is ever trimmed.
- **SPEC-SKY-004** — The orbit camera clamps pitch and distance, supports drag,
  wheel zoom, auto-rotation with a pause, and a reset that eases only tilt and
  distance back to the opening framing.
- **SPEC-SKY-005** — The native renderer draws the dome points, faceted terrain,
  horizon compass, stars, and a dish aimed at the live boresight in a single
  depth-tested GL pass. It must not crash when a GL context cannot be created;
  the page says the sky is unavailable instead.
- **SPEC-SKY-006** — The sky page shows translated obstruction legend and stats
  (obstructed fraction, observed hours), a waiting state before any survey
  arrives, and controls for rotation, reset, and showing or hiding unmapped
  sky.
- **SPEC-SKY-007** — A new survey replaces the dome data without tearing down
  the GL context, so the 30 s obstruction poll does not flash or rebuild the
  scene.

## Acceptance criteria

`dishy-sky` unit tests cover classification, both frames, envelope trimming,
camera bounds, and the generated vertex counts. The GTK integration test builds
the page, feeds it a synthetic obstruction reply, and checks the waiting state
gives way to the scene without a GPU. Opening the real view against a live dish
is the hardware check.

## Deferred

- The live Starlink constellation (TLE fetch, SGP4 propagation, serving
  candidate, trails, picking) is specified separately in 0101.
- Per-kit dish meshes: this milestone draws a procedural panel on a mast that
  aims at the reported boresight. The baked models for each hardware revision
  are a fidelity follow-up, not a prerequisite for reading the survey.
- The historical time-lapse scrubber needs the recorder's hourly snapshots to
  accumulate; the view reads the live survey first.
